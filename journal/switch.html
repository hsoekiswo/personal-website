Switch

August 2024

I decide to resign without another job in July.
Almost everyone is shocked by my decision.
Or perhaps, "disapprove" is more accurate description.
I thought this is because my decision is not responsible. But also most of them dying to do the same thing.

I'm not crazy. Here it is.
Let's disect those irresponsibilities.
There's a conventional wisdom discussion about priorities in life. The common one is health, relationship and money.
And most people comes up with those order:
- Health
- Relationship
- Money

The first priority is often put above, because it influenced the other two.
People who prioritize health think that health is the foundation to achieve good relationship and money.
But their everyday life can be completely different.
Everyday actions is done spontaniously. What they do is not based on their mind. Or it is, but your mind work pushed by fear.
Like the old sayings that "fear is the mind killer". So what we do can be the opposite what we think of.

I'm not saying that this is my ideal life, nor do I want to live like this forever. Instead, I'm really pragmatic. I know that in the next phase after this I will prioritize money over the rest again.

But this phase of my life is important. It's the chapter of relearning.
On how to prioritize health and relationship again.
So then when I need to prioritize money, I don't neglect my health and relationship completely.
